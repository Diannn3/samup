# Content Drafts Review

The following content drafts have been prepared based on the SAM-UP Constitution and current Bylaws, ensuring student-friendly, non-corporate language suited for an online tambayan.

1. **welcome-to-sam-up.md**: A short, warm introduction explaining the tambayan and pointing to verification.
2. **constitution-and-bylaws.md**: Summarizes the official identity, linking to the official Constitution (stable) and Bylaws (term-specific).
3. **server-guidelines.md**: Practical conduct expectations ensuring respect, privacy, and proper channel use without automatic bot enforcement.
4. **roles-and-membership.md**: Clear breakdown of Resident, Affiliate, and Applicant roles alongside ping roles.
5. **channel-directory.md**: A scannable list explaining where to post what.
6. **important-links.md**: Safe, public links for SAM-UP members.

These are stored in the `/content` directory for manual review. Nothing will be posted until explicit approval is granted.
