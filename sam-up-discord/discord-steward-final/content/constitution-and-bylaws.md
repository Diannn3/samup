# Constitution and Bylaws

Our organization is guided by two main documents:

1. **The Constitution**: This is our stable, long-term institutional document containing the core principles, objectives, and organizational structure of SAM-UP. It rarely changes and defines who we are as an organization.
   - 🔗 [Link to SAM-UP Constitution]

2. **The Bylaws**: This document contains the operational provisions for the current academic year (e.g., specific committee duties, fee structures, and current term guidelines). The Bylaws are regularly updated to reflect the evolving needs of the organization each term.
   - 🔗 [Link to Current SAM-UP Bylaws]

Please take the time to read them to understand how our organization operates!
