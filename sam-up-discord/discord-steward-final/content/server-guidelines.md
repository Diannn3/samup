# Server Guidelines

To ensure the SAM-UP online tambayan remains a comfortable, active, and welcoming environment for everyone, please observe the following guidelines:

1. **Be Respectful**: Treat all members with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and toxic behavior will not be tolerated. Keep the tambayan friendly!
2. **Use Channels Appropriately**: We have channels dedicated to specific topics (e.g., `#academic-help`, `#opportunities`). Please use the appropriate channels or forums for your discussions. Use `#random` for casual off-topic conversations.
3. **No Academic Dishonesty**: Do not share restricted assessment materials, leaked exams, or engage in answer-selling. Academic integrity is a core principle.
4. **Respect Privacy**: Do not share private organizational documents, meeting recordings, or personal information outside the server without explicit consent.
5. **No Spam or Impersonation**: Keep the chat clean and do not impersonate officers, members, or official university personnel.
6. **Keep Voice Rooms Clean**: When hanging out in the `Tambayan` or `Study Room`, be mindful of your background noise and use the appropriate voice channel for your activity.

**Note**: Human moderators (the Membership and Discipline Committee) enforce these rules. If you encounter any issues, please reach out to them!
