# Roles and Membership

Here is a quick overview of the roles you'll see in the server:

## Status Roles
- **@Unverified**: New joins. Can only access the `START HERE` category to complete verification.
- **@Member / @Alumni / @Applicant**: Staff-confirmed access roles that replace `@Unverified` and unlock the server areas appropriate to each membership status.

## Membership Roles
- **@Member**: Active resident and affiliate students currently enrolled.
- **@Alumni**: Graduated members of SAM-UP. 
- **@Applicant**: Current applicants with access limited to the areas approved for the applicant role.

Verification submissions are parsed by the bot, but an Admin, Executive, or Moderator selects the final access role.

## Staff Roles
- **@Executive / @Admin**: Current term officers managing the organization and the Discord server.
- **Moderators**: Members of the Membership and Discipline Committee tasked with maintaining order in the server.

## Optional Notification Pings
We also have optional roles you can grab in our channels to get pinged for specific things:
- `@Events Ping`
- `@Academic Ping`
- `@Opportunities Ping`
- `@Gaming Ping`
- `@Study Ping`
