# 🪧 Channel Directory

Welcome to the complete map of the SAM-UP Online Tambayan!

### 🚀 START HERE (Onboarding & Rules)
- `#welcome-to-sam-up` - Welcome message & onboarding steps.
- `#verification` - Open a ticket for bot-assisted parsing and staff role confirmation.
- `#server-guidelines` - Server rules and code of conduct.
- `#roles-and-membership` - Guide to server roles and access levels.
- `#channel-directory` - You are here! Complete map of the tambayan.
- `#arrivals` - Automated join notifications & new member arrivals.

### 📣 OFFICIAL (Announcements & Updates)
- `#announcements` - Official org-wide SAM-UP announcements.
- `#members-announcements` - Important announcements strictly for Members & Alumni.
- `#events-and-calendar` - Schedule and updates on upcoming SAM-UP activities.
- `#opportunities` - Internships, job posts, scholarships, and volunteer work.

### 🌻 TAMBAYAN (Community & Casual Hangout)
- `#gen-chat` - Main chat for anything and everything! Introduce yourself here!
- `#alum-chat` - Dedicated chat space for SAM-UP Alumni and Resident Members.
- `#random` - Off-topic discussions, random thoughts, and banter.
- `#memes-and-media` - Share memes, videos, photos, and TikToks.
- `#suggestions` - Feedback and suggestions for the Executive Committee.
- `#bot-commands` - Use bot commands here (music, games, tools).
- `#sports-night` - Sports watch parties and e-sports tournaments.
- `#sports-night-members-only` - Private sports discussion for Members & Alumni.

### 📚 ACADEMICS (Study Help & Resources)
- `#academic-help` - Ask questions about math courses, assignments, and concepts.
- `#academic-chat` - Casual chat about acads, professors, and study tips.
- `#resources` - Textbooks, notes, past papers, and study materials.
- `🔊 Acad Nights` & `🔊 Acad Nights 2` - Voice rooms for study sessions & tutoring.
- `🎙️ Acad Nights (Tambay Only)` - Stage channel for structured study lectures & reviews.

### 🎨 INTERESTS (Hobbies & Passions)
- `#gaming` - Find a party for Valorant, League, ML, Genshin, etc.
- `#music-and-movies` - Share music playlists, film recommendations, and reviews.

### 🔊 VOICE TAMBAYAN (Hangout Rooms)
- `🔊 Tambayan` - Main voice channel for casual hanging out & kwentuhan.
- `🛋️ Lobby` - General voice lounge for chilling before events or games.
- `📚 Study Room` - Quiet or focus study sessions.
- `🎮 Gaming Room` - Hop in when playing games with fellow members.
- `🎵 Music Room` - Listen to tunes together using music bots.
- `🎓 Alumni VC` - Voice hangout room for SAM-UP Alumni and Members.
- `🏅 Sports Night` - Voice channel for sports watch parties & tournaments.
- `🏅 sports night (members only)` - Private sports voice hangout for Members & Alumni.

### 🔒 PRIVATE STAFF
*Only visible to Executive Officers & Moderators.*
- `#moderator-chat` - Staff discussions & moderation coordination.
- `#verification-support` - Staff review area with Member, Alumni, and Applicant confirmation controls.
- `#server-logs` - Read-only automated audit trails & updates.
