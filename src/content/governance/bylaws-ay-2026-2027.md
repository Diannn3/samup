The A.Y. 2026–2027 Bylaws provide the organization's current operational layer beneath the Constitution.

For the public website, one especially relevant structure is the **Marketing Team**:

- the team operates as a unified team rather than separate permanent subteams;
- members contribute to both **Partnership** and **Promotions** responsibilities;
- two Deputy Heads oversee Partnership and Promotions respectively;
- Promotions includes creating content that promotes SAM-UP and its events and developing educational/promotional content that advances appreciation and understanding of mathematics;
- Partnership includes external relationship-building and approved collaboration responsibilities.

The website uses those public-safe functions to inform its **Partners** and **Explore Applied Mathematics** product areas.

The full bylaws contain operational procedures that should not be assumed public merely because they exist in the source document.
