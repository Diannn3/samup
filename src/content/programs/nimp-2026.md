The Department of Education issued **Advisory No. 008, s. 2026** for the National Invitational for Mathematical Prodigies (NIMP) 2026.

The advisory announced:

- **Date:** March 7, 2026
- **Venue:** UP Los Baños
- **Host:** Society of Applied Mathematics of the University of the Philippines Los Baños (SAM-UP)
- **Audience:** junior high school learners from public and private schools nationwide
- **Purpose:** to engage learners in activities that promote mathematical thinking and to recognize and cultivate mathematical talent

The announced competition coverage included junior-high topics across arithmetic, algebra, geometry, statistics, logic, word problems, and basic number theory.
